# Common errors mapping

> **Not documented** (same for every endpoint, or never surfaced over HTTP):
> - Authentication: `jwtDecodingError`, `invalidClaim`, `missingHeader`, `tokenVerificationFailed`, `badBearerToken`, `badDPoPToken`, `keyTypeNotAllowed`, `invalidJWKClaim`.
> - Middleware-only: —
> - Infrastructure: `authenticationSaslFailed`, `htmlTemplateInterpolationError`, `pdfGenerationError`, `genericError`, `thirdPartyCallError`, `tokenGenerationError`, `missingRSAKey`, `missingKafkaMessageData`, `kafkaMessageProcessError`, `jwksSigningKeyError`, `kafkaApplicationAuditingFailed`, `fallbackApplicationAuditingFailed`, `invalidSqsMessage`, `decodeSQSMessageError`, `pollingMaxRetriesExceeded`.
>
> Processes covered: `delegation-process`.

## `operationForbidden`

Insufficient privileges for the requested operation.

| Process | BFF endpoint | When it happens | Reachable from the FE? | Steps to reproduce (UI) | Resolution steps |
| ------- | ------------ | --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| `delegation-process` | `GET /delegations/:delegationId/contracts/:contractId` | The requester is neither the delegation's delegator nor delegate; `assertRequesterIsDelegateOrDelegator` in `getDelegationContract` throws after loading the delegation. The BFF handler has no ownership pre-check and forwards the caller to the process. | Cannot happen — the delegation list is filtered by the active tenant's `delegatorIds` or `delegateIds`, and `DelegationDetailsPage` offers contract download only for a delegation opened from that list. | — | — |

## `unauthorizedError`

The caller's roles are not authorized for the operation.

| Process | BFF endpoint | When it happens | Reachable from the FE? | Steps to reproduce (UI) | Resolution steps |
| ------- | ------------ | --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| `delegation-process` | `GET /delegations` | `validateAuthorization` in `getDelegations` throws when the caller has none of `ADMIN_ROLE`, `API_ROLE`, `SECURITY_ROLE`, `M2M_ROLE`, `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`, `SUPPORT_ROLE`, `REVIEWER_ROLE`, or `VIEWER_ROLE`. | Cannot happen — `DELEGATIONS` has `authLevels: ['admin', 'support']`, and both corresponding roles are admitted by the process handler. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `GET /delegations/:delegationId/contracts/:contractId` | `validateAuthorization` in `getDelegationContract` throws when the caller has none of `ADMIN_ROLE`, `API_ROLE`, `SECURITY_ROLE`, `M2M_ROLE`, or `SUPPORT_ROLE`. | Cannot happen — `DELEGATION_DETAILS` has `authLevels: ['admin', 'support']`, and both corresponding roles are admitted by the process handler. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /producers/delegations` | `validateAuthorization` in `createProducerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — admin is admitted by the process handler; for support, `DialogBasic` disables the final `Inoltra delega` confirmation through `SupportActionGuardProvider`, and the `Crea nuovo` entry point is also admin-only. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /producers/delegations/:delegationId/approve` | `validateAuthorization` in `approveProducerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — `useGetDelegationActions` renders the `Accetta` action only for an admin who is the delegation's delegate; admin is admitted by the process handler. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /producers/delegations/:delegationId/reject` | `validateAuthorization` in `rejectProducerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — `useGetDelegationActions` renders the `Rifiuta` action only for an admin who is the delegation's delegate; admin is admitted by the process handler. | — | — |

| `delegation-process` | `POST /consumers/delegations` | `validateAuthorization` in `createConsumerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — admin is admitted by the process handler; for support, `DialogBasic` disables the final `Inoltra delega` confirmation through `SupportActionGuardProvider`, and the `Crea nuovo` entry point is also admin-only. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /consumers/delegations/:delegationId/approve` | `validateAuthorization` in `approveConsumerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — `useGetDelegationActions` renders the `Accetta` action only for an admin who is the delegation's delegate; admin is admitted by the process handler. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /consumers/delegations/:delegationId/reject` | `validateAuthorization` in `rejectConsumerDelegation` throws when the caller has neither `ADMIN_ROLE` nor `M2M_ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — `useGetDelegationActions` renders the `Rifiuta` action only for an admin who is the delegation's delegate; admin is admitted by the process handler. | — | — |
| `delegation-process` | `DELETE /consumers/delegations/:delegationId` | `validateAuthorization` in `revokeConsumerDelegation` throws unless the caller has `ADMIN_ROLE`; the BFF forwards the UI Authorization header without a role pre-check. | Cannot happen — the revoke confirmation in `DialogRevokeDelegation` is disabled for support users, and admin is admitted by the process handler. | — | — |

## `hyperlinkDetectionError`

Hyperlinks are not allowed in the supplied text.

| Process | BFF endpoint | When it happens | Reachable from the FE? | Steps to reproduce (UI) | Resolution steps |
| ------- | ------------ | --------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| `delegation-process` | `POST /producers/delegations/:delegationId/reject` | The rejection reason contains a hyperlink; `validateNoHyperlinksSafe` in `rejectDelegation` (called by `rejectProducerDelegation`) throws `hyperlinkDetectionError`. The BFF forwards the reason unchanged. | **CAN HAPPEN** — the rejection form validates only that the reason is 10–250 characters and does not remove or reject hyperlinks. | **Data precondition:** You are an admin of tenant B, the delegate on a producer delegation from tenant A, and the delegation is waiting for approval.<br>1. Go to `/aderente/deleghe` and open the _Deleghe ricevute_ tab.<br>2. Open the waiting producer delegation from A.<br>3. Click _Rifiuta_.<br>4. Enter `https://example.com` in _Motivazione_ and click _Rifiuta delega_. | 🟢 **Easy resolution**<br>1. Remove the hyperlink from the reason and submit a plain-text reason of 10–250 characters. |

Go to `/aderente/deleghe` and open the _Deleghe ricevute_ tab.<br>2. Open the waiting consumer delegation from A.<br>3. Click _Rifiuta_.<br>4. Enter `https://example.com` in _Motivazione_ and click _Rifiuta delega_. | 🟢 **Easy resolution**<br>1. Remove the hyperlink from the reason and submit a plain-text reason of 10–250 characters. |
